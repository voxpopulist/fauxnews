Whether you have come from north or south, east or west,
near or far, every leader in this beautiful hall today
represents a rich culture, a noble history,
and a proud heritage that makes each nation majestic
and unique, unlike anything else in human history
or any other place on the face of the earth.
From London to Lima, from Rome to Athens,
from Paris to Seoul, from Cairo to Tokyo and Amsterdam
to right here in New York City,
we stand on the shoulders of the leaders and legends,
generals and giants, heroes and titans,
who won and built our beloved nations, all of our nations.
