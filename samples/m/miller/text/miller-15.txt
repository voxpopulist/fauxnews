Erica stands on the shoulders of thousands of years
of warriors, of women who raised up families,
raised up city, raised up industry,
raised up civilization, who pulled us out of the caves
and the darkness into the light.
The light will defeat the dark.
We will prevail over the forces of wickedness and evil.
They cannot imagine what they have awakened.
They cannot conceive of the army that they have arisen in all of us.
